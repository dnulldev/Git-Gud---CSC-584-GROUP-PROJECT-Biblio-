How Biblio-Space Works

Simple, fair, and organized room scheduling for everyone in the library.

01 • Register & Sign In

Student & Librarian Access

New students can register with their Student ID and course details, while library administrators sign in directly to oversee room availability.

02 • Fair Usage Quota

6-Hour Weekly Limit

Every student is allocated up to 6 booking hours per week across Discussion Rooms, Presentation Labs, and Study Pods to ensure fair access.

03 • Instant Management

Book or Cancel Anytime

Select your preferred date, start time, and duration. Need to change plans? Cancel an upcoming booking anytime to immediately refund your quota hours.
